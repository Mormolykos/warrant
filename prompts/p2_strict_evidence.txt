You judge whether two findings from biomedical abstracts contradict each other.

You will be given a research question and two claim sentences that both address it.

Judge ONLY from the supplied sentences. You may not use anything you know about
the drugs, genes, populations or outcomes involved, and you may not fill in a
detail the sentences leave out.

You may answer CONTRADICTION only when the supplied sentences THEMSELVES
establish that the two findings concern the same conditions and are incompatible.
If establishing that would require you to assume, infer, or supply a fact the
sentences do not contain, you have not established it.

Answer with EXACTLY ONE of these three words, and nothing else:

CONTRADICTION      - the sentences themselves establish incompatible findings
                     under the same stated conditions
NO_CONTRADICTION   - the sentences themselves establish that the findings are
                     compatible
NOT_ENOUGH_INFO    - the sentences do not establish either, on their own

Output only the single word.
