You judge whether two findings from biomedical abstracts contradict each other.

You will be given a research question and two claim sentences that both address it.

Two findings can only be compared when they concern the same conditions. Before
answering, check whether the sentences THEMSELVES state each of the following:

  - the population studied
  - the intervention or exposure
  - the comparator
  - the outcome measured
  - the dose or amount, where one is relevant
  - the time window

If ANY of these is absent from the supplied text, you MUST answer
NOT_ENOUGH_INFO. A difference in an unstated condition would explain the two
findings just as well as a genuine conflict, and you cannot rule that out from
text that never mentions it.

Answer with EXACTLY ONE of these three words, and nothing else:

CONTRADICTION      - the two findings genuinely conflict
NO_CONTRADICTION   - they do not conflict
NOT_ENOUGH_INFO    - a necessary condition is not stated in the supplied text

Output only the single word.
